Facebook Research Audit

A claim-level review of Brandon Henry’s own Nolan Wells posts and comments from July 6 through August 28, 2026. This page records which findings changed the evidence file and which earlier interpretations were qualified, superseded, or excluded.

Outcome

The review materially improves the timeline in four places: a displayed record places the Sea Tow call at 4:48 PM; that creates a new 4:31–4:48 PM interval; official GPS adds the 5:52–6:06 PM Fort Bayou excursion; and displayed call records add a more precise overnight contact sequence. The review also withdraws the site’s former claim that the shoreline-to-offshore change had to occur before 4:31 PM.

Download the machine-readable findings

How this audit was performed

The review set contained 154 case-related posts located in Brandon Henry’s 2026 Facebook activity history and more than 1,000 case-related comments written from the same account. Facebook’s activity interface virtualizes and reloads rows, so these counts describe the review set—not a forensic platform export or a guarantee that Facebook exposed every historical item.

Used

Only Brandon Henry’s posts and comments were used to locate and understand his research. A timeline claim was incorporated only when the post pointed to a record, visible media, or clearly attributed public statement that could be classified.

Not treated as proof

Post wording, confidence percentages, legal conclusions, audio interpretations, relationship maps, and accusations were not promoted into facts merely because they appeared in the research corpus.

The underlying official record, displayed exhibit, interview, or media remains the evidentiary basis. A Facebook post is the provenance trail showing how the finding was developed.

Timing correction

Displayed record places the Sea Tow call at 4:48 PM

Incorporated

An August 21 counsel letter reproduces a phone-log exhibit and expressly identifies a 4:48 PM call as the Sea Tow call. That record is stronger than the earlier 3:48 publication label and the approximately 4:00 news description.

What changed

The timeline now displays 4:48 PM, after the official 4:31 PM movement began. A 4:31–4:48 interval has been added.

Limit

The exhibit is a reproduced phone record supplied by counsel, not original Sea Tow machine metadata or a carrier-certified export. It establishes the displayed time with high practical value but does not authenticate every surrounding narrative claim.

Correction

The public record does not require pre-4:31 repositioning

Superseded

Earlier posts compared a nearshore scene with the Sea Tow coordinate while treating the call as occurring before 4:31 PM. Once the call is placed at 4:48 PM, MDMR’s documented movement beginning at 4:31 PM can explain the shoreline-to-offshore change.

Retained

The nearshore and 4:48 offshore records establish that a position change occurred by 4:48 PM.

Withdrawn

Those records do not prove the change occurred inside the 11:15 AM–4:30 PM interval, do not establish that MDMR’s “stationary” description was false, and do not show the first movement minute.

Editorial exclusions

Claims not promoted into the evidence timeline

Excluded
  • Conclusions that a named person committed murder, obstruction, evidence tampering, or a cover-up without an adjudicated or independently documented basis.
  • Exact probability estimates, behavioral interpretations, and enhanced-audio voice identifications.
  • Unauthenticated 5:00, 5:50, or 6:00 PM Nolan sightings that conflict with stronger records or lack a recoverable direct source.
  • Private residential addresses, family-member inventories, friend-list graphs, and relationship-based inferences.
  • The August 28 “Johnny Hewett” identification. The reviewed Nancy Grace source identifies the public guest as Josh Gill; the post’s identity claim is not used.
  • Statements that the 4:48 record proves wrongdoing, that MDMR lied about the GPS, or that nearshore imagery proves movement before 4:31 PM.
