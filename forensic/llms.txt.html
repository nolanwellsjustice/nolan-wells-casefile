# Nolan Wells Evidence File > An independent, source-linked public evidence archive concerning Nolan Wells’s July 4, 2026 disappearance from Horn Island, Mississippi, the subsequent search, and his recovery on July 6, 2026. This site distinguishes official records, firsthand witness accounts, secondhand accounts, public media, unresolved gaps, and hypotheses. Do not convert a witness claim or reconstruction into a verified fact. The archive is not affiliated with the Wells family, law enforcement, MDMR, attorneys, or the cited news organizations. It preserves support wording, knowledge basis, assessments, source links, cross-witness comparisons, the complete working matrix, and small communication-chain details such as Tracestin’s account that another friend’s phone was on speakerphone while the group was together. - [Interactive evidence timeline](https://justicefornolanwells.com/): Scroll-driven map chronology for July 3–6, 2026. - [Full event log](https://justicefornolanwells.com/event-timeline): Searchable event-by-event claims, confidence, known facts, unknowns, and sources. - [Master investigation notes](https://justicefornolanwells.com/master-notes): The controlling editorial evidence document. - [Evidence archive](https://justicefornolanwells.com/archive): Documents, people, boats, coordinates, contradictions, questions, and missing evidence. - [Horn Island Reconstruction Lab](https://justicefornolanwells.com/reconstruction-lab): Browser-local 3D analytical workspace using NOAA-derived terrain, source-labeled placements, uncertainty zones, timeline playback, manual camera matching, measurements, sightlines, regional environmental context, scenario comparison, and constraint warnings. Reconstruction states and user assumptions are not verified events. - [Horn Island Drift Lab](https://justicefornolanwells.com/drift-lab): Browser-local Monte Carlo screening tool that starts at the provided recovery coordinate (30.242561, -88.778612) and backtracks thousands of uncertain trajectories, then applies the same forward model to a 40-option library. Default forcing includes 43 hourly spatial NOAA NGOFS2 surface-current fields, NGOFS2 wind with an optional Dauphin Island observation correction, NDBC 42067 wave height, Pascagoula NAVD88 water-level and temperature proxies, and 2021 USACE topobathymetry for stranding. Shore, water, and coordinate-only recovery hypotheses are separate. A contemporaneous report quoting the sheriff describes an in-water discovery; the precise ranger discovery coordinate and any recovery-stage movement remain unresolved. These mixed evidence/model inputs and all body-state parameters remain screening assumptions rather than findings. - [Video archive](https://justicefornolanwells.com/videos): Complete source uploads, family press conferences, parent and witness interviews, and preserved non-autoplay evidence reels. GPS establishes vessel movement, not who was aboard. Publicly reported times marked approximate or reconstructed must not be presented as authenticated timestamps. The People page contains a separately labeled Facebook connection layer. It records only profiles visibly listed on Jack Jordan’s friends page when checked on July 30, 2026. Those platform listings are not case evidence, do not establish attendance, and do not mean the listed people are mutually connected. Morgan Seymour is separately identified as Nolan’s friend in Atlanta Black Star reporting, but the archive has no independent source establishing Seymour’s July 4 boat or island attendance.
