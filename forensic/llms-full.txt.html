# Nolan Wells Evidence File — Extended Guide Canonical site: https://justicefornolanwells.com/ Repository: https://github.com/brandonhenry/NolanWellsCaseFile Last reviewed: 2026-09-09 Coverage: Core events July 3–6, 2026, with source-provenance and research-audit records through September 9, 2026; Horn Island and Ocean Springs, Mississippi ## Scope and independence The Nolan Wells Evidence File is an independent public evidence archive. It is not an official investigative record and is not affiliated with Nolan Wells’s family, attorneys, witnesses, law enforcement, the Mississippi Department of Marine Resources, the U.S. Coast Guard, Sea Tow, or any cited news organization. The archive’s purpose is to make the public record easier to inspect while keeping facts, reported recollections, media-derived claims, unknowns, and interpretation visibly separate. ## Witness Claim Ledger The [Witness Claim Ledger](https://justicefornolanwells.com/witness-claims) converts the working witness-evidence matrix into 208 searchable entries across 23 named people or attributed accounts. Each card keeps the proposition, source-of-knowledge, support wording, assessment, topic, and available source link separate. Prose-only witness sections are represented as individual claim cards, while the complete matrix remains available as a formatted context view. Small communication-chain details are retained atomically; for example, Tracestin’s transcript account is separated into the call to another friend’s phone, the phone being on speakerphone, the group being together, the conversation with Warren through that device, and Warren ending the call to dial the Coast Guard. The ledger measures support for propositions, not honesty, guilt, or criminal responsibility. ## Horn Island Reconstruction Lab The [Horn Island Reconstruction Lab](https://justicefornolanwells.com/reconstruction-lab) is a browser-local analytical workspace, not an official reconstruction. It uses a compact derivative of the 2021 USACE NCMP Mississippi topobathymetric DEM in EPSG:6345 and NAVD88/Geoid12b, with separately colored historical NOAA/NCEI bathymetry used only where the 2021 surface has no data. Remaining gaps stay explicit. The lab preserves source classification separately from placement certainty; verified measurements, strongly constrained placements, approximate placements, scenario assumptions, and computed conflicts are visually distinct. The immutable evidence baseline and editable working scenarios include only explicit keyframes. MI1295CB/Tideline waypoints are never converted into a continuous track. GPS establishes vessel movement rather than passenger identity. Camera matching is manual, environmental readings are labeled regional context, and JSON exports omit locally attached media bytes. ## Horn Island Drift Lab The [Horn Island Drift Lab](https://justicefornolanwells.com/drift-lab) is a browser-local Monte Carlo screening model. It begins at the provided recovery coordinate (30.242561, -88.778612) and samples recovery-coordinate and recovery-time uncertainty before backtracking particle origins through July 6, July 5, and July 4. A contemporaneous WLOX report quoting Sheriff John Ledbetter says a park ranger found the body in the water near the northwest end around 8:45 AM; the coordinate was separately provided as the shore location. Without the ranger report, the exact first-discovery coordinate and any recovery-stage movement remain unresolved. Users therefore choose coordinate-only, stranded-on-shore, or still-in-water recovery criteria. Each particle samples space/time-varying current and wind, wave sensitivity, horizontal diffusion, leeway, floating or submerged state, bottom influence, shoreline contact, and possible decomposition-related refloating. The same engine independently propagates particles forward from every checked candidate entry. Its 40-option library is organized into source-linked anchors, an 11-point west-tip time sweep, a 10-point 4:30 PM direction/distance sweep, and 13 later-entry alternatives through July 6. Seven core comparisons are selected initially so the page remains responsive; an entire group or all 40 tests can be selected at once. Default forcing includes 43 hourly spatial NOAA NGOFS2 surface-current fields, NGOFS2 wind with an optional Dauphin Island observed-vector correction, NDBC 42067 observed wave height, Pascagoula observed NAVD88 water level and water temperature as spatial proxies, and 2021 USACE topobathymetry for shoreline contact. Horn Island Pass `ps0201`, Ship Island `gp0101`, and `MOB1105` harmonic current predictions are retained as point cross-checks, not substituted for the spatial field. The displayed percentage is the fraction ending inside the selected recovery criterion; later-entry tests are labeled as shorter-window diagnostics because their percentages are not directly comparable to July 4 entries. The model is not SAROPS, a medical conclusion, or a validated expert opinion, and the supplied coordinate still does not resolve the sequence of discovery, wash-up, or movement during recovery. The body-state ensemble uses a deliberately wide 65–150 accumulated-degree-day decomposition/refloat bracket around the limited two-case forensic literature. At the observed Pascagoula proxy temperature of roughly 30°C, that corresponds to approximately 52–120 hours. It therefore generally falls after the 41.75-hour July 4, 3:00 PM to July 6 recovery interval; sink/refloat particles mostly remain submerged or bottom-influenced during that window. This is an explicit sensitivity limit rather than case-specific biological validation. ## Evidence vocabulary The [Dispatch Audio Timeline](https://justicefornolanwells.com/dispatch-audio) is a separate source-review layer produced after a complete automated transcription pass across the 115-file July 4–6, 2026 Jackson County Public Safety and MSWIN scanner archive. Its timestamps are derived from archive file-name starts plus audible segment offsets, not agency CAD clocks. The feed combines multiple agencies, so no transmission is attributed to JCSO or another department unless the audio establishes it. Clips never autoplay, unclear words remain bracketed, and the machine-readable ledger preserves original and derivative SHA-256 checksums. - Verified: Supported by an obtained official record, official audio, authenticated primary media, or another direct authoritative source. - Firsthand witness: A named person’s account of what that person says they personally saw or heard. - Secondhand: A person or publication relaying somebody else’s account. - Media record: A public image, audio file, or video whose visible or audible content is available but whose original metadata may be incomplete. - Unknown: A documented gap the available public evidence does not resolve. - Hypothesis: An interpretation or reconstruction that is not itself a verified event. Confidence is separately labeled High, Medium, or Low and includes a written reason. ## Defensible public chronology - July 3, 2026: Nolan leaves home and stays with friends before the Horn Island trip.
